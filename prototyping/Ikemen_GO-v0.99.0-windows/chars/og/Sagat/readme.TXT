==============Movelist===============

Here's how moves will be notated:

LP - Weak punch
MP - Medium punch
HP - Strong punch
LK - Weak kick
MK - Medium kick
HK - Strong kick
P - Any punch
K - Any kick
==============Special Moves==============
*Tiger Shot - D, DF, F + P
*Tiger Shot (crouching) - D, DF, F + K
*Tiger Uppercut - F, D, DF + P
*Tiger Knee - F, D, DF + K
==============The Super Combo==============
*Tiger Genocide - D, DF, F, D, DF, F + P
==============Play as Old Sagat==============
To play as Old Sagat, hold Start and press any punch or kick. For those
of you who don't know who Old Sagat is, he's the pre-Turbo version of himself.
No Super Combos, no multi-hitting Tiger Uppercut, faster Tiger Shot...sorry I couldn't
make the means of selecting Old Sagat true to Super Turbo - it's a Mugen limitation,
I guess.
